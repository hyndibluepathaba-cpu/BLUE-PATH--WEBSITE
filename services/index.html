Blue Path ABA is a leading provider of therapy services for children with autism and related needs. Our team of experienced professionals works closely with each child and their family to develop a personalized treatment plan that promotes skill acquisition, addresses behavior challenges, and enhances overall development.

Our therapy programs are overseen by Master’s level, Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) who continually monitor progress and adjust treatment plans as needed. In addition, we have a team of highly trained and skilled Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) who implement the treatment plans and provide one-on-one therapy to our clients.

RBTs play a critical role in the success of our therapy programs, as they work directly with the children and their families to ensure that the treatment plans are being implemented effectively and efficiently. Our RBTs receive extensive training and ongoing supervision from our BCBAs, and are held to the highest standards of professionalism and clinical expertise.

At Blue Path ABA, we take a collaborative and individualized approach to care, and are committed to delivering the highest-quality clinical services to every child we serve.

All therapy programs receive the following:

  • A comprehensive, in-depth assessment

  • Individualized treatment plan

  • Behavior plan development

  • Training outline for parents, caregivers, therapists, aides and teachers
