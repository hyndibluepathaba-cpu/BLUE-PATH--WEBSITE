About Us

Blue Path ABA Cares

Blue Path ABA was founded to establish a child-guided, positive and effective therapeutic environment for children with autism and related needs. Our program uses ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) as the treatment methodology to help all of our clients reach their maximum level of independence and ability.
